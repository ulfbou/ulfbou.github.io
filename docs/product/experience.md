# Portfolio experience

Visitors first encounter a concise point of view, then choose a facet or browse all work, scan project proposition/status/technology, and expand a project without losing page context. Expanded detail shows story, contribution, reflection, artifact, evidence, and limitations.

Selection changes emphasis, never truth. Essential meaning cannot depend on interaction. Controls are native, keyboard-usable, and expose state. Motion is restrained and optional. Tone is precise, reflective, technically specific, transparent about maturity, and free from unsupported promotion.

The portfolio is curated rather than exhaustive. Counts of repositories, lines, files, packages, workflows, or evidence paths are never quality scores.

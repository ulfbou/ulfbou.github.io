# Platform delivery roadmap

## Status and authority

This is the single active public delivery roadmap for `ulfbou.github.io`. It replaces the portfolio-only roadmap as the controlling delivery sequence while preserving accepted portfolio outcomes below as completed or continuing portfolio foundations.

The roadmap governs delivery order and public outcomes. It does not replace:

- `docs/platform.md` for platform product direction;
- area-specific product documents for landing-page, portfolio, article, or learning meaning;
- `docs/publication.md` for publication authority and gates;
- `docs/architecture/static-composition.md` for composition architecture;
- `docs/contracts/routes-and-output-ownership.md` for route and emitted-path ownership.

Accepted roadmap work is not evidence that the corresponding route, output, workflow, or deployment behavior is already implemented.

## Delivery principles

Every milestone and delivery must:

- identify affected public routes and normalized output ownership;
- preserve the applicable semantic authority;
- distinguish verified current behavior from accepted target behavior;
- preserve accepted portfolio content, evidence boundaries, interaction, accessibility, compatibility, and failure behavior;
- declare required automated and manual validation evidence;
- preserve public URLs or provide an explicitly owned compatibility decision;
- exclude private, candidate, diagnostic, local, and unpublished material;
- stop when a mandatory result, ownership claim, approval, or compatibility obligation is unknown or failing;
- retain one human-authorized release boundary;
- keep optional evidence, automation, analytics, and AI assistance outside the runtime prerequisites of already published material.

Acceptance Ready means the bounded outcome is implemented, every mandatory check has named evidence, limitations and compatibility effects are explicit, required public content is approved, and no blocking defect or unknown result remains.

Done means the result is accepted, public documentation matches implementation, semantic non-regression passes, the exact artifact is authorized where release is in scope, and no undocumented workaround remains.

**Outcome:** Independently appropriate public areas are assembled through a governed publication plan into one validated static artifact.

- Composition starts from an empty destination.
- Path normalization rejects absolute, drive-qualified, malformed, traversing, concealed, ambiguous, and conflicting paths.

### Definition of Done

- The final inventory traces emitted files to admitted owners.
- The release record traces the final artifact to admitted units and source revisions.

- Ulf authorizes the exact identified artifact for release.

## Preserved portfolio foundations

The following accepted portfolio outcomes remain required and must not regress during platform delivery.

### Dynamic portfolio vertical slice

The portfolio has an established one-page editorial model with structured JSON loading, facet filtering, in-place project expansion, project proposition, story, contribution, reflection, status, technologies, artifacts, evidence, limitations, loading, failure, empty, responsive, keyboard, focus, zoom, reduced-motion, and deployment-oriented behavior.

Its original completion criteria remain portfolio requirements:

- obsolete template content is absent;
- visible claims are approved or explicitly representative;
- experimental work is not presented as production-ready;
- placeholder contact information, private data, and invented metrics are not public;
- accessibility and responsive reviews have named results.

### Approved structured portfolio content

Approved source under `src/content/` deterministically produces the retained generated portfolio projection under `src/Ulfbou.Site/wwwroot/data/generated/`.

The portfolio contracts continue to require:

- profile, project, facet, technology, artifact, evidence, link, status, reflection, limitation, provenance, visibility, reading depth, and publication meaning;
- stable unique identities and resolved references;
- exclusion of candidate and private content;
- non-zero failure for duplicate identities, unknown values, malformed metadata, and dangling references;
- semantically equivalent output from equivalent approved input;
- reusable project presentation rather than project-specific Razor branches;
- one authority and traceable approved source for every public portfolio fact;
- valid and invalid fixture coverage;
- no hand-editing of generated data as an alternative authority.

### Versatile portfolio story blocks

Portfolio stories may use admitted prose, quote, reflection, code, terminal, image, links, evidence, callout, relationship, replay, and diagnostic-comparison blocks according to `docs/contracts/story-blocks.md`.

The preserved outcomes include:

- stable block identity, type, accessible label, reading depth, provenance, order, publication metadata, and validated payload;
- publication failure for unknown types or missing renderer coverage;
- selectable code and terminal text;
- meaningful image alternatives;
- quotation attribution;
- separation of reflection and evidence;
- coherent use of multiple block types;
- desktop, mobile, keyboard, zoom, reduced-motion, and print behavior;
- content-only addition of ordinary projects;
- preservation of established compatibility fields and presentation.

### First solid public portfolio

The portfolio remains intended to stand independently of future automation. It must communicate Ulf’s direction, present a coherent curated project trajectory, and preserve purpose, contribution, competence, learning, maturity, evidence, limitations, explanatory artifacts, accurate metadata, responsible links, concise overview, and progressively disclosed detail.

Ulf approves portfolio content and visual result. Unsupported strengthening, dead links, private data, and quantity-as-quality scoring remain prohibited.

### Portfolio evidence package contract

Repository observations may enter portfolio review without becoming claims. Versioned contracts cover repository and revision identity, scope, observations, candidates, evidence, technologies, warnings, exclusions, provenance, visibility, and compatibility.

Evidence packages contain no layout and grant no publication authority. Candidate, reviewed, approved, and published states remain distinct. Partial results identify missing evidence. The portfolio must continue to work without evidence packages.

### Deterministic portfolio collector

A future bounded collector may produce one reviewable evidence package when a demonstrated need justifies it. Repository, revision, project, sink, inclusion, exclusion, normalization, binary, generated, oversized, sensitive, missing, and conflicting-input policies must be explicit.

The collector must not judge quality, infer contribution, write final public prose, change approved content, or obtain publication authority. Equivalent governed inputs produce semantically equivalent output, and mandatory failures return non-zero.

### Optional bounded portfolio curation

Optional evidence-assisted curation may produce reviewable suggestions. Suggestions retain candidate identity, task, evidence, revision, configuration, original value, review state, decision, and accepted edit. Accept, edit, and reject behavior must be explicit.

Unreviewed and rejected output remains absent from public content. Unsupported claims and sensitive inputs are blocked. Deterministic evidence remains distinct from probabilistic suggestion. Direct publication is prohibited, and the core portfolio works without curation.

### Fixture-backed portfolio evidence prototype

Before automated collection, a repository-local fixture prototype proves authority separation with one proposed claim fixture, one revision-addressed evidence package, one explicit public reference, and one deterministic evidence-index projection.

The fixture is not approved portfolio content. The prototype does not alter the site, deployment, approved-source scope, route ownership, or platform composition.

Its preserved gates include:

- machine-readable inference disablement;
- two-layer public visibility;
- package identity over scope and policy;
- independent schema and compiler-contract versions;
- stable invalid-fixture diagnostics;
- no new Python or NuGet dependency;
- no repository secret;
- no scheduled execution;
- no operational orphan branch;
- no deployment change;
- no approved-source implementation or acceptance claim;
- only the admitted workflow action identities already documented by the repository;
- no external service, paid dependency, or mutation-capable pull-request workflow.

## Cross-roadmap non-regression

Every milestone blocks on:

- semantic loss, contradiction, incorrect direction, or overstated maturity;
- weakened qualifications, limitations, attribution, provenance, or authority;
- unreviewed publication or private exposure;
- broken route, asset, build, deployment, interaction, accessibility, direct-loading, no-JavaScript, responsive, or static behavior within the affected scope;
- duplicate, ambiguous, unsafe, stale, partial, or unauthorized output;
- dependency on optional later tooling;
- unsupported promotion of evidence, metrics, generated text, or runtime data into public meaning;
- confusion between current implementation and accepted target behavior;
- unknown mandatory validation.

Temporary duplication of governing detail is preferable to information loss. Information may be transferred or removed only when its complete operational meaning, constraints, exceptions, compatibility, validation, failure behavior, authority, and status are preserved in an accepted destination.

## Overall Definition of Done

The platform provides one coherent public identity across a professional landing page, a complete portfolio, durable technical articles, and learner-centred AWS Cloud and AI experiences. Each area retains appropriate semantic authority and experience design.

One platform-owned publication plan allocates normalized emitted paths. Clean composition produces one identified static artifact. Validation establishes output ownership, privacy, integrity, accessibility, compatibility, and representative public behavior. Deployment publishes the validated artifact without changing it.

The portfolio remains strong without evidence collection or probabilistic assistance. Articles remain authored works rather than generated activity logs. Learning remains comprehension-led rather than assessment-first. Ulf remains final authority for public meaning and release.
